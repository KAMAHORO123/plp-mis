## Try the new workflows

1. As admin, upload a learning material to the schools in **Learning materials**. As club leader, download it and mark it completed. As admin, inspect **Reports & analysis** with the school filter.
2. As club leader, publish an activity with its date/details/photos. As member, browse **Clubs & communities** and click Participate. See the reminder on the member dashboard. As club leader, on the activity date, record attendance and submit the report. Admin can review it with photographs.
3. As club leader, select school members in **School certificate requests** and submit. No programme/evidence is entered. As admin, open the request table, request corrections or approve. After correction/resubmission, its status is Corrected until reviewed. Approved records support individual and batch PDF downloads.
4. As admin, create a course name and assign a trainer. As trainer, prepare lessons, assignment and assessment, attach documents, save and publish. Members enroll and download the documents.
5. As member, prepare an opportunity application and attach an optional CV, cover letter or supporting letter.

Member signup is immediate. Staff role requests remain unprivileged until assigned. The coordinator demo is assigned to the Kigali school. The old standalone Club leader demo has been removed; old stored leader profiles migrate to the school-coordinator role.

Only the public website has the automated FAQ. Staff profiles have account details rather than the Youth Passport. Community discussions, invitation creation, project entry and reward redemption are no longer active sections.

Uploads and certificates are frontend demonstrations, not shared files or official credentials. See [current specification](current-workflows.md) and the root README for backend requirements.
