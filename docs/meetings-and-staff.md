# Staff and meeting setup

Applicants choose Trainer, Mentor or Club leader during signup and verify their email. Verification leaves staff applications pending. Administrators approve or decline them in **Members & staff**. Pending accounts receive no dashboard session; they see only an "Application under review" page. In **Members & staff**, each pending applicant has an **Approve** button (and a check-mark icon in the account table), and every account row has edit, suspend/reactivate and delete icons. Suspending an account revokes its sessions; backend permissions enforce access independently of browser state. **Add account** creates a pre-approved account; the person sets a password with *Forgot password*. Accounts with learning, attendance or authored records cannot be deleted — suspend them instead.

Only approved trainers create courses; administrators cannot create, assign or edit course content. In **Content studio → Course publishing** an administrator sees submitted courses read-only and can publish, unpublish or request changes. Approved trainers use **Course studio** to build a course in five steps — overview (outcomes, skills, pathway), modules (a reading and a timed knowledge check each), assessment (graded assignment brief and a timed final exam with pass mark and attempt limit), learner preview, and publication checklist — then submit it for publication. The trainer dashboard shows published/draft counts, enrolled learners, completion rate, assignments to review, a next-steps list, and per-learner quiz, assignment and exam results. Administrators publish or request changes. Trainers see their own courses and learners and provide assignment feedback. Server-backed published courses award completion certificates and credits. Enrolled curriculum is locked; create a new course version for changes.

Approved mentors land on the **mentor dashboard**: next session, this week's agenda, tabs for requests, upcoming sessions, sessions needing notes, history, their mentees and notifications sent. They accept (with an HTTPS link or a typed venue), reschedule, decline, and record held sessions; each change is saved on the server before the screen updates. Mentors edit their public profile (headline, focus area, bio, photo) and can pause new requests, which the server enforces. This workspace is absent from the administrator sidebar. Sessions are stored on the server, and notification recipients come from the participant records.

Administrators open **Meetings** (`/meetings/manage`) to adjust the date, times, venue, credits and attendance radius. The form states the device timezone. Publishing places a check-in link in the public banner. No monthly date is assumed or automatically published.

The default venue is ALX Deco Center, KG9 Avenue, Kigali. The pin (-1.9270514, 30.0986973) comes from the Google Maps directions linked in the [official ALX hub directory](https://onboarding.alxafrica.com/pathway/hubs/). Confirm it on site and adjust if necessary.

Check-in requires a verified account, an open attendance window, a fresh browser location within the venue radius including its accuracy margin, and the host code. Display the code at the venue and rotate it when necessary. Google Maps provides directions; browser geolocation supplies the location signal. Location can be spoofed: these checks reduce remote claims but cannot guarantee physical attendance. Use supervised check-in for stronger assurance.

Credits are awarded once per account per meeting in a database transaction. Repeated or simultaneous submissions cannot duplicate them. The register stores distance and accuracy, not exact participant coordinates. Credit amounts are locked once attendance exists. HTTPS is required for browser geolocation except on localhost.

Assessments on trainer-built courses are enforced by the platform API: correct answers are never sent to learners, attempts are timed on the server (answers save as they are chosen, and only answers saved before the deadline count), three tab departures submit an attempt, and failed attempts are limited per 24 hours. Modules complete only by passing their quiz (80%); the course completes, issues the certificate and awards credits only after every quiz, a 60-word assignment and the final exam are passed. Courses published before this change keep their content readable but show as "being updated" until the trainer adds module quizzes and a final exam.

File storage: the platform stores uploads in `UPLOAD_DIR` (a relative path is resolved inside `services/platform`; local development uses `services/platform/uploads`, which is git-ignored; Docker uses the persistent `uploads` volume at `/app/uploads`). Members may store 50 MB (documents and images up to 10 MB); trainers and administrators 5 GB, including MP4/WebM lesson videos up to 500 MB, streamed with byte ranges. Profile pictures are PNG or JPEG up to 2 MB, visible to signed-in members.

Course videos: each module can have a YouTube video (embedded via youtube-nocookie.com) or an uploaded recording, previewed in the builder and shown above the module reading. Uploaded videos are readable only where their course is readable.

Proctored assessments: module quizzes and final exams require the learner's camera (shown as a live preview, never recorded or uploaded) and open in full screen. While an attempt is open the learner cannot navigate to other pages, and closing the tab prompts a warning. Leaving the tab, leaving full screen or turning off the camera counts as a departure; three departures submit the attempt. If the camera is off on return, questions stay hidden until it is turned back on while the timer keeps running. These checks deter casual cheating but run in the browser; they are not a guarantee of identity. Opportunities and applications are platform records: administrators choose whether members apply through PLP or on an external https link. Members see only certificates issued to them. Deploy migrations through `017_user_avatars.sql`, then restart the platform API and mail worker. Route `/api` to that service. Set `VITE_HUB_URL` on a separate landing deployment so meeting links open the member Hub. Root `src` and mirrored `frontend/src` include these changes. The credits page reads the server ledger; browser-only legacy actions do not count as verified awards. Administrator bootstrap remains an explicit deployment step.
