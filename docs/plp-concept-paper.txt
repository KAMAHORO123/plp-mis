PEACE AND LOVE PROCLAIMERS

Concept Paper for the PLP Hub Platform
Prepared for: PLP RCA
1. Executive Summary
The PLP Hub is envisioned as the official digital learning, talent development, membership
management, and opportunity platform for Peace and Love Proclaimers (PLP).
The platform will serve as the backbone of the Discover & Develop Stage of the PLP Pathway,
enabling young people to discover their potential, build practical skills, earn certifications, access
opportunities, and prepare for the next stages of their PLP journey.
The PLP Hub should function as a combination of:
● Learning Management System (LMS)
● Youth Development Platform
● Talent Management System
● Community Platform
● Leadership Academy
● Opportunity Marketplace
● Digital Portfolio System
The platform should be accessible through both:
● Web Application
● Mobile Application (Phase II)
