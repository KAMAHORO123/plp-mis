# Landing page photography

The source images in `public/images/plp/` were extracted from the user-supplied PLP organization portfolio. Mirrored assets live in `frontend/public/images/plp/`.

On September 28, 2026, nine active landing-page photographs (workshop, walk, academy, community, dialogue, remembrance, arts, sober-nights, and megacare) were restored using the built-in AI image editor and encoded as 2048-pixel-wide WebP files. The AI returned approximately 1348–1541-pixel-wide images, subsequently resized to 2048 pixels; these are AI-enhanced and resized images, not native 2K camera originals. Fine faces, lettering, and other details can differ from the source. Pre-replacement files for both asset folders are preserved in `output/imagegen/before-2k/`. The exact prompt and generated source paths are recorded in `output/imagegen/2k-manifest.json`. The existing 3840-pixel-wide youth hero remains in place. Unused portfolio assets were not replaced.
