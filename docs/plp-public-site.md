- Impact: reported achievements are separated from the portfolio's 2026 targets. No targets are described as completed results.
- PLP Hub: one component of Discover & Develop; public course previews remain at `/courses`, while member routes retain their access guards.

The repeated Hub feature grid and generic benefits copy have been removed from the organization pages. Programme photographs are paired with captions identifying their portfolio context.
